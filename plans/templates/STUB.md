# STUB M-nn — ‹the milestone's name, as a claim or a question›

*A stub is a future milestone's full context, written so a fresh mind with no conversation memory can plan it with all meaning
intact (the stub rule, `CLAUDE.md` § The pipeline). Copy this file to `plans/STUB-<slug>.md`; Poros writes § The question and the
bearings, Ephodos's record job writes new stubs, Keryx files banners. A demoted stub keeps its text under a banner saying why.*

## The mission, in plain language

‹What the milestone is for, why now, and what the deciding session knew: the observations, the results it starts from (by row and
grokking), the dead ends and why they died.›

## Definitions, stated once

‹Every term the question uses, with its source address — the claim list will quote these.›

### The card

‹A question about every solution, in the most general class where it makes sense, that cannot be answered without an invariant every
solution carries (`CLAUDE.md` § Method, the invariant question) — or "no structural form yet", and why.›

‹Only where an outcome decides what runs next; each scored at the record job.›

‹Stated honestly: what an answer would change about the problem, and what it would not.›
