# Ephodos — The Invention

The Ephodos core carries the cut between what an argument carries and what its sentence conveys, and its recognition companion finds the field where an object's description already has a theory. This skill is for the case neither covers: every available description has been tried, each fails on the same line, and the line is a premise none of them earned. Then the description has to be made. *Invenire* is to come upon, to find — and "invent" has also meant, since the sixteenth century, to make up, to fabricate. The skill lives on that edge. What the canon calls its leaps were almost never made up: each was built from held parts by changing one premise, after long work had shown that nothing else would do, and each was secured by derivation before it was believed. The core states the discipline in a sentence; this skill spends the words, because premise revision is where a model is weakest and most fluent at once. Measured in 2023, models' performance "substantially and consistently degrades compared to the default conditions" when a task's default premises are changed; a 2026 benchmark of counterfactual physical laws found that "minimal perturbations cause a sharp decline in symbolic accuracy"; and ideas judged more novel than experts' at proposal lose more than theirs when they are carried out `[t½:~12mo]` — stamps carrying the core's epoch. A model will state a revised premise and then reason from the old one; it will call a daring sentence a result.

The whole skill serves one terminal test: **the premise remade and secured.** Not a bold idea, and not a new vocabulary with the old assumptions underneath, but one premise located, revised by the least change that clears the collision, and shown — by a model, by the recovery of everything the old premise had earned, and by a consequence that could have failed — to carry what it claims. This standard has its lineage. ὑπόθεσις is "that which is placed under", and Riemann called his lecture on the foundations of geometry "Über die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen" — on the hypotheses that lie at geometry's foundations. Einstein wrote of the problem he solved in 1905 that every attempt had failed "as long as the axiom of the absolute character of time, or of simultaneity, was rooted unrecognized in the unconscious. To recognize clearly this axiom and its arbitrary character already implies the essentials of the solution of the problem." Lobachevsky, of the parallel postulate: "The fruitlessness of the attempts made, since Euclid's time, for the space of 2000 years, aroused in me the suspicion that the truth, which it was desired to prove, was not contained in the data themselves." And Dedekind made the paradox Galileo had dismissed into a definition — "A system S is said to be infinite when it is similar to a proper part of itself" — and named where the work lay: others had seen the property, but "neither of these authors made the attempt to use this property for the definition of the infinite and upon this foundation to establish with rigorous logic the science of numbers, and just in this consists the content of my wearisome labor". The procedure works inside a posed question and revises the premises of the theory the question is posed in: an axiom, a definition, a law's domain, the range of admissible objects. When what must change is the question itself — the unknown, the condition, what counts as done — it goes back to whoever holds the frame. And when the revised premise defines a word the question uses, the question's sense has moved with it: answer in the new sense, say so, and let the poser decide whether it was the question asked. Drive toward the secured premise deliberately; the procedure below is how.

<the_one_cut>
Every technique here serves a single discrimination: **earned versus inherited.**

A premise is *earned* when something carries it: a derivation, a measurement, or the question's own condition. It is *inherited* when it is held only because every available description holds it — because the words in which the problem is posed were built on it, and nothing else. A premise is earned as far as its receipt reaches, and no further: for a derivation, as far as what its argument uses; for a measurement, the regime measured; for the question's condition, the question. Past that reach the same premise is inherited, and the collision usually lives exactly there. The whole is greater than the part for every collection that ends, by an induction on its size, and for no other collection by anything. The core's cut asks whether a conclusion conveys more than its argument carries; this cut asks it of a premise, one step upstream: is it carried by anything at all, or only conveyed by every description in sight?

The cut decides the move and marks the failure in both directions. The move is to let something earned overrule something inherited. Einstein's simultaneity had no receipt but habit; the relativity principle and the constancy of light had experiments and a tested theory behind them. So simultaneity gave way, "durch Definition", and the two postulates stood. The failure one way is letting an inherited premise overrule an earned result: Saccheri derived the consequences of denying the parallel postulate and rejected them — in Bonola's words "trusting to intuition and to faith in the validity of the Fifth Postulate rather than to logic", on an argument "founded upon the extension to infinity of certain properties which are valid for figures at a finite distance", a premise carried past its reach. The failure the other way is licence: an earned premise given up for the sake of novelty. For years Einstein tried to give up the constancy of light, which Maxwell's tested theory carried — "which I first believed had to be given up. Only after years of [jahrelang] groping did I notice that the difficulty lay in the arbitrariness of basic kinematical concepts."

Three mechanisms make the cut hard. The inherited premise is the natural one: it is "rooted unrecognized", and Norton's diagnosis of a thought experiment that blamed the wrong premise is that "the real culprit ... escapes because it is so natural". The setting chooses the suspect: a scenario built in the old description points at the premises the description makes visible, and "the setting of the thought experiment almost irresistibly draws us to the wrong target". And for a model the inherited premise is the training distribution's default, which is exactly where measured performance falls when a premise is changed: the revision is stated in one sentence and the default is used in the next.

*Inherited* is a finding, not a default. Its receipt is the showing: the derivations that failed to produce the premise from what is earned, the demonstration that no measurement fixes it, or the reach of its receipts computed and found short of the case. The canon showed it every time — two thousand years of failed proofs, a concept "not fixed by experience", an interpretation proving the postulate independent. Until it is shown, a premise is unsorted, and an unsorted premise is not revised; a receipt you cannot recall is not a receipt you have shown missing. Before revising an inherited premise, name what it has been doing: the earned results that use it are the ones the securing must recover. Earned is not untouchable; it is revised only on evidence of its own, and last.
</the_one_cut>

<the_collision>
Begin from the collision, never from the failure. "The proof does not go through" is a mood; a collision is a datum. The canon's leaps began from four kinds.
- **Two held descriptions both apply and disagree.** Einstein's 1905 paper opens with Maxwell's electrodynamics applied to a magnet and a conductor, which "zu Asymmetrien führt, welche den Phänomenen nicht anzuhaften scheinen" — leads to asymmetries that do not seem to belong to the phenomena. The current depends only on the relative motion; the description split one case in two.
- **A derived consequence the frame refuses.** Dirac's equation allowed negative energies, and he declined the easy exit: "we cannot simply reject by a new assumption. We must find some meaning for these states." His reason, given of another consequence in the same lecture: "one must believe in this consequence of the theory, since other consequences of the theory which are inseparably bound up with this one ... are confirmed by experiment." Bombelli found Cardano's imaginary roots "more sophistic than true", until he "searched hard and found the demonstration". Planck, in 1931, called the quanta of 1900 "a purely formal assumption", and for years held them, in Kragh's account, "a kind of mathematical hypothesis". Each is the core's second failure — a consequence carried and not conveyed — and the leap is its correction.
- **A proof or a construction that will not close.** Two thousand years of attempts on the parallel postulate; Hamilton's want of a multiplication of triplets, which had "haunted" him "for at least fifteen years" — and in the October it yielded, his sons' question every morning at breakfast, "Well, Papa, can you multiply triplets?" A proof that fails the same way for long enough is evidence about its premises.
- **A paradox dismissed.** Galileo matched every integer with its square, saw that the squares are fewer below any bound, and concluded that "the attributes 'equal,' 'greater,' and 'less,' are not applicable to infinite, but only to finite, quantities" — the paradox put out of bounds by withdrawing the concepts. Dedekind, two and a half centuries later, made it the definition.

State the collision in the words of the descriptions that collide: description A gives this, description B gives that, both apply here, and here is the case. Then earn the licence to revise a premise, because a revision without one is the licence the cut forbids. The licence is the ledger of ordinary moves tried and killed. Norton on Einstein: he "could know that his novel and outrageous thought was the right one of the many possible because of the thoroughness of his preparation. It showed him that nothing else would work." The simultaneity move "entered only in the final five to six weeks of his seven or more years of work", as "the adoption of a new approach to an old problem to which he was compelled by the failure of all other avenues." In this identity the ledger is the recognition companion's killed homes, each killed on a named line of its dictionary. Try the existing homes before minting a new object: Dirac first read the hole in his negative-energy sea as a proton, and made the positron only when mass and annihilation killed that reading.
</the_collision>

<the_thought_experiment>
The instrument that most often exhibits a collision is the thought experiment, and it is where the most fluent mistakes are made, so it has its own discipline: rewrite it as an argument before believing it. Norton's thesis is that thought experiments "are merely picturesque argumentation": their knowledge "comes from premises introduced explicitly or tacitly into the thought experiment", transformed "through deductive or inductive argumentation" into the result. Mach said what they do well — their basic method is "die Methode der Variation", varying the circumstances to see which are "maßgebend", determining — and endorsed Duhem's warning against presenting them as physical experiments, "also Postulate für Tatsachen auszugeben": that is, passing off postulates as facts.

So write every thought experiment as numbered premises and a conclusion, the natural premises first, and sort each premise earned or inherited. Its conclusion carries what its weakest premise carries. It is then one of three things.
- **A derivation,** when every premise is stated and earned. Newton, projecting his cannonball from a mountain, stated the idealization and its reason: "to keep up the parity of cases, let us suppose either that there is no air about the Earth, or at least that it is endowed with little or no power of resisting."
- **A finding device,** when a premise is a guess or a picture: what it finds must be demonstrated afterwards, by Archimedes' rule. Galileo's tied stones are one, and a secured one: the reductio needs a second premise, that two stones tied together fall at a speed between their separate speeds; its conclusion is scoped — bodies fall alike "provided they are of the same specific gravity" — and his text sets a reported test beside it. Einstein called his pursuit of a light beam "a paradox upon which I had already hit at the age of sixteen", built from three held things — what experience shows, what Maxwell's equations allow, and that uniform motion cannot be detected — and in 1916, asked whether he then believed light's speed constant, answered "No, it was just curiosity." Rewritten as an argument, Norton finds, it tells against the emission theories of light, not the ether theory it is usually aimed at, and carries a constraint on any successor: keep what "the most secure part of Maxwell's theory" earned. Its culprit took a decade to find.
- **A smuggler,** when the picture hides a premise. Norton names the two ways: "Hasty acceptance of idealizations" and "Hasty generalization" — "we may be induced to accept assumptions, hidden in the picturesque scenario, that we would never accept were they made explicit in a less picturesque environment."

A thought experiment finds the collision and lists the candidate culprits. It does not choose among them; the sort of `<the_premises>` does, and the choice is secured only by `<the_securing>`.
</the_thought_experiment>

<the_premises>
List every premise of the colliding descriptions on the page, one line each: the premise; *earned* or *inherited*; the receipt. A list held in the head keeps the premises you already doubt and drops the one doing the work. Take them from everywhere they hide: the axioms and definitions of each description; the idealization and its regime; the quantities held fixed; the objects admitted; the picture of every thought experiment in play, rewritten; and the vocabulary itself, since a word can carry a premise that no sentence states.

Put the natural premises first, because they are where the culprit hides. Then sort each with its receipt. The questions that sort are the ones the canon asked. Is it fixed by experience? — Norton's reading of Einstein's debt to Hume and Mach is that the traditional concept of simultaneity "was not fixed by experience; and that its use had tacitly committed us to a false presumption." Is it contained in the data? — Lobachevsky's suspicion was that the parallel postulate "was not contained in the data themselves." Is it a property of the problem, or of the objects to hand? Hamilton wanted a multiplication obeying the law of moduli, the modulus of a product equal to the product of the moduli: that was the question's own condition, earned. Commutativity was a property of the numbers he already had, not of the problem, and it was the premise that gave way.

The collision runs through the inherited premises among those that together produce it — often one that every colliding description shares, sometimes one that one description holds and another contradicts, sometimes one on each side, each used past the reach of its receipt. Revise one at a time, the least first, and secure each. When a revision secured alone leaves a residue, the residue names the next premise, and the pair is secured together, with the order kept in the ledger: Hamilton's anticommuting triplets left a remainder that was exactly the square of a missing coefficient, and admitting a fourth unit was the second premise to go. When every premise in play is earned inside its reach, the collision is the world's: stop, and take the honest exit.

Once the culprit is named, search once more before revising it — in the words its revision would carry: its negation, the object it would admit, the definition it would need. The homes recognition killed all shared the premise, because they came from the neighbourhood of the costume; a field that has already revised it is found only in the revision's words, and those words exist as soon as the culprit is named. Found, it is a home after all, and recognition's transfer carries it across. For a model this is the likeliest case.
</the_premises>

<the_revision>
Choose the least revision that clears the collision. Hamilton, testing a triplet algebra: "Behold me therefore tempted for a moment to fancy that ij = 0. But this seemed odd and uncomfortable, and I perceived that the same suppression of the term which was de trop might be attained by assuming what seemed to me less harsh, namely that ji = − ij." The harshness of a revision is how much of what was earned it gives up: ij = 0 would have broken the law of moduli, the question's own condition, for the product of i and j, whose moduli are 1; ji = −ij gave up only commutativity. Bohr kept ordinary mechanics for the stationary states and suspended it for the passage between them — the stationary states "can be discussed by help of the ordinary mechanics, while the passing of the systems between different stationary states cannot be treated on that basis" — and suspended classical radiation inside the atom altogether. Einstein kept Maxwell's theory for bodies at rest. The least number of premises, not a new world: Lobachevsky's geometry, in Bonola's description, was "founded upon the said 28 propositions and on the negation of the Fifth Postulate" — Euclid's propositions that never use it, kept whole.

Then state the revision in one of the forms the canon used, because each has its own receipt.
- **A definition,** replacing a concept that had no receipt: simultaneity "durch Definition"; Dedekind's infinite. This is not the core's forbidden repair of redefining a term so that a counterexample does not count: a definition here makes the paradox its content and says more, and its model and its consequence show it.
- **A negated postulate,** its consequences derived as far as they go: Bolyai and Lobachevsky, after Saccheri and Lambert.
- **A constructed object,** built from held parts, in which the stuck statement holds: the imaginary numbers; ideals where Kummer had ideal numbers; Hamilton's k, which was the residue of the failed calculation — "the square of the coefficient of k" — taken as a unit rather than suppressed.
- **An impossibility promoted to a principle:** Stevin's chain; the relativity principle raised "zur Voraussetzung", to a presupposition.
- **The refused consequence, conveyed:** Dirac's hole, with the properties the formalism fixed and the old description could not state.
- **A held law suspended in a named place,** with its boundary stated: ordinary mechanics, suspended in Bohr's transitions.

A revision that must be supplemented by an exception for each new case has not found the premise; a definition written so that the stuck statement comes out true by fiat, with no model and no consequence, is monster-barring in reverse. Lakatos: "Lemma-incorporation increases content: monster-barring does not." The test of a revision is that it says more than before, not that it makes the problem go away.
</the_revision>

<the_derivation>
Derive under the revision, and expect most of the work to be here. Dedekind's "wearisome labor"; Bolyai's appendix; Einstein's nine sections after the definition. The derivation is written as the core writes any: entries with their *uses only*, *not claimed*, and model, each stage a relay-result a stranger could resume from.

The characteristic failure is the counterfactual drift: the revised premise is stated and the inherited one is used, silently, somewhere downstream. It is the failure measured on models when a task's default is changed, and it is invisible to the reader of the steps, because every step reads naturally — natural is exactly what the old premise is. The guard is a control whose planted fault *is* the old premise. Each control on a consequence of the revision is run a second time with the inherited premise put back, wherever the two disagree inside the regime the control computes, and it must fail then; if it passes with the old premise planted, it was not reading the revision. Hamilton's letter runs the test in one direction — with ji = −ij, what is left over in the triplet product is exactly the square of k's coefficient — and the control runs the other: with commutativity restored, as ij = ji = 0, 1, or −1, the law of moduli fails in each case. Where the two premises agree inside the regime a control computes, planting the old one changes nothing and the control cannot fire: the recovery always, since the recovery is where the old premise holds, and every finite check of a revision about the infinite. There, the recovery's control plants a case outside the regime where the old premise was earned, and the guard against the drift is the argument itself — each step read for a use of the old premise, and the record saying it was read.

Name the *not claimed* line with care here, because a made description invites the widest over-claim there is: the revision is a claim about the theory the question is posed in, within the regime its evidence reaches — not about the world, not about every theory, and not about the question someone else might have asked.
</the_derivation>

<the_securing>
A made description is secured by four receipts, each written down, and until all four are in hand it is a conjecture, graded as one.
- **A model of the new premises inside held theory.** An object that satisfies every revised premise, built from theory already trusted, so that the new premises are consistent if the old theory is; for a law suspended in a regime, the model sits inside mathematics already trusted, not inside the physics the law replaced. Bonola, of the geometry that denied the parallel postulate: "the fact that contradictions have not been met is not sufficient to prove this; we must be certain that, proceeding on the same lines, such contradictions could never be met" — and the certainty came from an analytic interpretation, whose formulae "give the proof of the independence of Euclid's Postulate from the first principles of geometry", and from Beltrami's surfaces. A constructed object carries its model by construction. This is the core's *model* line, at the scale of a theory.
- **The earned results recovered where the old premise held.** Everything the old premise had earned must come back, inside the regime where it was earned: the new description reduces to the old one there. Norton's principle, of any successor to Maxwell's theory: "The new theory can only deviate from it in realms in which Maxwell's theory has not been thoroughly tested." For collections that end, the whole is still greater than the part.
- **A consequence no earlier description could state, and that could have failed.** Bohr's formula gave the factor of the hydrogen series from the charge and mass of the electron and Planck's constant; with his constants it comes to 3.1·10¹⁵ s⁻¹ against the observed 3.29·10¹⁵, and the factor goes as the inverse cube of Planck's constant, so a factor of two in that constant would have missed by eight. Dirac's hole had the electron's mass and the opposite charge, and could annihilate. A consequence that could not have failed, or that was fitted after the fact, is not this receipt; the core's "prediction the derivation was not built to fit" is the guard. For a definition this receipt measures what the definition lets one derive, not truth about the world.
- **The collision dissolved.** The original datum is now a consequence, not a contradiction: of the magnet and the conductor, "daß die in der Einleitung angeführte Asymmetrie ... verschwindet" — that the asymmetry of the introduction vanishes. Hamilton checked his product against the question's own condition, "without which consistence being verified, I should have regarded the whole speculation as a failure." If the collision survives in a new form, the revision moved the difficulty without removing it.

The four receipts are the cut applied to the revision itself. The model shows the old premise was independent of everything earned, which is the proof of its sort; the recovery keeps what was earned; the new consequence earns the new premise a receipt of its own; the dissolution shows the collision ran through what was never earned.

The signal fails in known ways. A model proves consistency relative to held theory, never truth of the world; which geometry is physical was handed to measurement. The heuristic that found a result can be false while the result stands, so a result is no evidence for the picture that produced it. And the sense of revelation proves nothing about priority or truth: Bolyai wrote to his father "I have created a new universe from nothing" in a letter that says, just before, "I have not yet completed this work", and Gauss answered the finished work that its contents "coincide almost entirely with my meditations, which have occupied my mind partly for the last thirty or thirty-five years."
</the_securing>

<the_look_back>
- **Wonder is no wonder.** Stevin's title page carried the motto "Wonder en is gheen wonder", and Mach glossed it: "every enlightening progress made in science is accompanied with a certain feeling of disillusionment." Once secured, the made description should make the old paradox look obvious. If the result still seems to contain more than its premises, find where the premise already contained it.
- **Search again, in the new words.** A made description is as likely to be second as a recognized one — Gauss's thirty-odd years — and the finished revision has more words to search by than its culprit had.
- **Grade it, in both directions.** Not above what the four receipts carry — a revision with a model and no checked consequence is a consistent conjecture. And not below: a revision graded "formal" when its receipts are in hand conveys less than it carries, and the next derivation will not build on it. A model plowing ahead without naming what it made is the same failure; one mathematician's report on machine solutions is that "the human authors often develop useful new objects, terminology, etc. to capture what they're doing, while the models usually just plow ahead". Name the object the revision made, define it, and let the next derivation use it.
- **Write down the ledger.** The collision, the premise list with its sorts, the killed homes, the revisions tried and their costs: the next collision will run through a premise already on the list.
</the_look_back>

<read_the_ugliness>
Each of these is the invention reporting that it is being faked, and each names where to go back to.
- **A new vocabulary with no premise changed.** Recombination in the costume of a revision. Which premise changed, and what does the new description forbid that the old allowed?
- **A revision proposed before the ordinary moves were exhausted.** No licence. Back to `<the_collision>`; write the ledger.
- **An earned premise given up.** Licence, not invention. Name its receipt; revise the inherited one first.
- **A premise sorted inherited because its receipt was not recalled.** No showing, no sort.
- **A revision that defines a word of the question, unannounced.** The question's sense moved. Say so, and let the poser decide.
- **A premise revised before a search in the revision's words.** The home may already be in print.
- **A thought experiment believed from its picture.** Rewrite it as an argument, the natural premises first.
- **The culprit chosen because the scenario pointed at it.** The setting's suspect. Sort every premise before choosing.
- **A definition that makes the stuck statement true and says nothing else.** Fiat. It needs a model and a consequence that could fail.
- **An exception added for each new case.** The premise has not been found. Back to `<the_premises>`.
- **The revision stated and the old premise used downstream.** The drift. Plant the old premise as a control's fault.
- **No model of the new premises.** Consistency unshown; a conjecture, graded as one.
- **Something the old premise earned, lost.** The revision was too harsh. Recover the limit or revise less.
- **A consequence fitted after the fact, offered as a prediction.** Not the third receipt.
- **"A new universe", before the derivation or the search.** Declared, not secured. Derive it; search in the new words.
- **A consequence called meaningless without a derivation that it is.** The refused surplus. It may be the finding.

Push harder on none of these. Return to the premise list with the smell as the sharper instruction.
</read_the_ugliness>

<the_loop>
Invention is a loop over the premise list: state the collision → check the licence, the ledger of killed moves → rewrite every thought experiment as an argument → list the premises, the natural ones first → sort each, earned or inherited, with its receipt → name the inherited premise the collision runs through → search in the words its revision would carry → choose the least revision and state it in a canonical form → derive under it, with the old premise planted as a fault → secure it by the four receipts → look back. Each pass either secures a revision or kills one with the receipt that failed, and adds it to the ledger. It ends at a **state**: the premise remade and secured, written as derivation entries with their controls, or the honest exit — the contradiction stated among named premises, each sorted, the culprit undecided, handed on as a no-go, which is a result. It never ends at the first revision that removes the contradiction, because removing the contradiction is the easiest receipt and the least.

Three stops bracket it. The economic stop: a revision whose securing costs more than the result is worth is said so, with the arithmetic, and the call is the principal's. The dispatch: a secured revision goes to its second mind (`ephodos-verification`) and then to the arbiter that can rule on it — the kernel for a definition's consequences, the experiment for a law's; when every shared premise is earned, the collision is the world's, and it goes back to whoever holds the frame as a question that must change. And the reopening: a new collision in the made description or a failed prediction reopens the same ledger; the made object found already in print goes to recognition's transfer, and the ledger records that it was found.

The win is the least revision that makes the collision a consequence — one premise, changed where it was never earned, with everything earned kept — and nothing added to feel revolutionary. The canon's inventions look small from inside: a definition of simultaneity, a sign in a product, the negation of one postulate.
</the_loop>

<commands>
The gate is the core's — `../ephodos/scripts/derive_gate.py`, relative to this skill, standard library only. An invention is filed as derivation entries and controls, as the core files any result, and one block that names which of them is which receipt:
```text
invention: <name>
  revises: <the inherited premise, as the premises section lists it>
  licence: <address of the ledger of killed moves>          premises: <address of the sorted premise list>
  model: <entry or address, or none — reason>               recovery: <entries, or none — reason>
  consequence: <entries, or none — reason>                  dissolved: <entry or address, or none — reason>
  old premise planted: <a control that fires, or none — why no control in the regime can plant it>
```
The gate resolves every field: the addresses exist, the premise revised is one the premises section lists, the receipts named are derived entries, and the control named fired on its fault. It prints the grade the receipts carry — *secured*, when all four are present, or *a conjecture*, naming the ones a `none` line says are missing — and it does not grade a record that fails. Run it on the invention document, from this skill's directory:
```bash
python3 ../ephodos/scripts/derive_gate.py all INVENTION.md   # the entries, the controls re-run, the invention block resolved and graded; non-zero exit on any failure
```
